Add streak freezes. Each user gets 2 per calendar month (UTC). On a habit's
detail page, a missed past day in the history grid gets a "Use freeze" action
next to it; a frozen day counts toward current and longest streaks but not
toward completions, the 7-day count or the 30-day rate. Show "Freezes left
this month: N" on the detail page. A habit with no frozen days behaves
exactly as before, and the acceptance checks still pass.
